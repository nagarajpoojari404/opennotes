Probability distributions are categorized into discrete (countable outcomes) and continuous (unbroken range of outcomes).

Discrete Distributions

1. Bernoulli Distribution

Models a single trial with only two possible outcomes: success (x=1x = 1x=1) with probability ppp, or failure (x=0x = 0x=0) with probability 1−p1 - p1−p.

  • Probability Mass Function (PMF): P(X=x)=px(1−p)1−x,x∈{0,1} P(X = x) = p^x (1 - p)^{1 - x}, \quad x \in \{0, 1\} P(X=x)=px(1−p)1−x,x∈{0,1}
  • Parameters & Moments: Mean μ=p\mu = pμ=p, Variance σ2=p(1−p)\sigma^2 = p(1 - p)σ2=p(1−p)
  • Example: Tossing a biased coin once where landing heads has probability p=0.7p = 0.7p=0.7.
