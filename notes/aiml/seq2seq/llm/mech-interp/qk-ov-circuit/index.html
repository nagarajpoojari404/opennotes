Mechanistic Interpretability
QK OV circuit

QK OV circuit

Mechanistic interpretability treats neural networks not as black boxes, but as computational graphs where weights and activations can be reverse-engineered into human-understandable algorithms.

In the mathematical framework popularized by Anthropic (Elhage et al., A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits), an attention layer is analyzed without the intermediate token dimensions by factoring it into two independent, end-to-end operations:

  1. The QK (Query-Key) circuit: Determines where information moves.
  2. The OV (Output-Value) circuit: Determines what information moves.

The Residual Stream as a Communication Bus

In a decoder-only autoregressive Transformer, token representations live in an nmodeln_{\text{model}}nmodel​-dimensional space called the residual stream (x∈Rdmodelx \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}}}x∈Rdmodel​).

Layers read from the residual stream via linear projections and write back into it by addition:

xl=xl−1+∑h=1Hheadl,h(xl−1) x_{l} = x_{l-1} + \sum_{h=1}^{H} \text{head}_{l,h}(x_{l-1}) xl​=xl−1​+h=1∑H​headl,h​(xl−1​)

A standard multi-head attention head hhh uses four weight matrices:

  • WQ,WK∈Rdk×dmodelW_Q, W_K \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k \times d_{\text{model}}}WQ​,WK​∈Rdk​×dmodel​
  • WV∈Rdv×dmodelW_V \in \mathbb{R}^{d_v \times d_{\text{model}}}WV​∈Rdv​×dmodel​
  • WO∈Rdmodel×dvW_O \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}} \times d_v}WO​∈Rdmodel​×dv​

Usually, we think of attention sequentially:

  1. qi=WQxiq_i = W_Q x_iqi​=WQ​xi​, kj=WKxjk_j = W_K x_jkj​=WK​xj​
  2. Ai,j=softmaxj(qi⊤kjdk)A_{i,j} = \text{softmax}_j\left(\frac{q_i^\top k_j}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)Ai,j​=softmaxj​(dk​​qi⊤​kj​​)
  3. vj=WVxjv_j = W_V x_jvj​=WV​xj​
  4. head(x)i=WO∑jAi,jvj\text{head}(x)_i = W_O \sum_j A_{i,j} v_jhead(x)i​=WO​∑j​Ai,j​vj​

Mechanistic interpretability collapses the internal dimension (dkd_kdk​ or dvd_vdv​) into direct end-to-end bilinear maps on the residual stream.

1. The QK Circuit: Directing Attention

The attention score between query token at position iii and key token at position jjj (ignoring positional embeddings for a moment) is:

scorei,j=qi⊤kjdk=(WQxi)⊤(WKxj)dk=xi⊤WQ⊤WKxjdk \text{score}_{i,j} = \frac{q_i^\top k_j}{\sqrt{d_k}} = \frac{(W_Q x_i)^\top (W_K x_j)}{\sqrt{d_k}} = \frac{x_i^\top W_Q^\top W_K x_j}{\sqrt{d_k}} scorei,j​=dk​​qi⊤​kj​​=dk​​(WQ​xi​)⊤(WK​xj​)​=dk​​xi⊤​WQ⊤​WK​xj​​

We define the QK matrix as:

WQK=WQ⊤WK∈Rdmodel×dmodel W_{QK} = W_Q^\top W_K \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}} \times d_{\text{model}}} WQK​=WQ⊤​WK​∈Rdmodel​×dmodel​
  • Rank: WQKW_{QK}WQK​ has a maximum rank of dkd_kdk​ (typically 64 or 128), which is much smaller than dmodeld_{\text{model}}dmodel​ (often 4096+). It acts as a low-rank bilinear form.
  • Interpretation: Given a feature subspace active in the query token (xix_ixi​) and a feature subspace active in the key token (xjx_jxj​), WQKW_{QK}WQK​ directly computes their compatibility score:
scorei,j∝xi⊤WQKxj \text{score}_{i,j} \propto x_i^\top W_{QK} x_j scorei,j​∝xi⊤​WQK​xj​
  • Function: It acts as an addressing system. The QK circuit reads queries and keys from the residual stream and outputs scalar logits over context positions, entirely agnostic to the semantic content being copied or transformed downstream.

2. The OV Circuit: Transforming Content

Once attention weights Ai,j∈[0,1]A_{i,j} \in [0, 1]Ai,j​∈[0,1] are computed, the head moves information from source position jjj to destination position iii. Looking at a single source position jjj:

contributionj=WO(WVxj)=(WOWV)xj \text{contribution}_j = W_O (W_V x_j) = (W_O W_V) x_j contributionj​=WO​(WV​xj​)=(WO​WV​)xj​

We define the OV matrix as:

WOV=WOWV∈Rdmodel×dmodel W_{OV} = W_O W_V \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}} \times d_{\text{model}}} WOV​=WO​WV​∈Rdmodel​×dmodel​
  • Rank: Like WQKW_{QK}WQK​, WOVW_{OV}WOV​ has a rank bounded by dv≪dmodeld_v \ll d_{\text{model}}dv​≪dmodel​.
  • Interpretation: WOVW_{OV}WOV​ is a linear map that reads a low-rank subspace from the residual stream at the source position and writes another low-rank subspace directly into the residual stream at the destination position.
  • Function: It dictates what features get extracted from the source and in what direction they are written into the target. Crucially, WOVW_{OV}WOV​ has zero influence on which positions are attended to—it simply specifies the payload.

Why Factorize into QK and OV?

  1. Separation of Concerns: When analyzing trained models, an attention head can fail or succeed in two distinct ways:
    • Addressing failure: Looking at the wrong token (QK circuit issue).
      • Transmission failure: Looking at the right token but writing the wrong vector or projecting into an orthogonal subspace (OV circuit issue).
  2. Eigenvalue/SVD Analysis of WOVW_{OV}WOV​:
    • If WOVW_{OV}WOV​ has positive real eigenvalues, the head acts as an identity/copying head, reinforcing existing token features.
      • If it has negative real eigenvalues, it acts as an inhibition head, erasing or dampening certain directions in the residual stream.
  3. Composability Across Layers: In multi-layer models, the output of an early head’s OV circuit feeds into the input of a downstream head’s QK or OV circuit. This leads directly to induction heads and attention composition pathways (QQQ-composition, KKK-composition, and VVV-composition).
